## Step 2: Professional Audit 4 (Presentation)
Once you receive a positive readiness decision, you can take part in Professional Audit 4.

### Format
- Team size: Pairs (2 students)
- Duration:
  - 15–20 minutes presentation
  - 15 minutes Q&A with evaluators
- Type:
  - Oral presentation with slides
  - Code review of project and test files

### Project Scope
You will present one of the following projects:
- Java: buy-02
- Rust: local-host  
**The audit is evaluated individually, even though the presentation is done in pairs.**

### Audit Objective
The goal of Professional Audit 4 is to evaluate your ability to:
- Analyze a software system from a testing and quality perspective
- Design and reason about manual and automated test strategies
- Work with test environments, including Continuous Integration (CI)
- Reflect on collaboration, documentation, and code quality
- Communicate testing decisions and results clearly

This is not a live coding session.  
Focus on reasoning and decisions. 

### Audit Structure (What Your Presentation Must Cover)
Your presentation must be structured around the following themes:
- Project overview and collaboration
- Requirements and documentation
- Code review and quality control
- Testing strategy and test planning
- Test environments (including CI)
- Manual vs automated testing
  - Automated testing is mandatory. If the project does not already include automated tests, students are expected to create and present them as part of the audit.
- Reporting and results  
**Both team members are expected to actively participate. Read the full brief here.**

### Evaluation and Outcome
After the audit:
- If you pass, a positive decision is signed at the next Progress Ceremony
- If you do not pass:
  - you may request to receive feedback
  - you must complement the presentation
  - you will be invited to a future audit session
